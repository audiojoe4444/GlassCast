GlassCast

Send to your glasses

Paste a podcast's RSS feed link (a private Patreon feed works too) or type a show name. It appears on your glasses in a few seconds.

On the glasses: Search → Add via phone. Your phone remembers the code.
Tip: tap Share → Add to Home Screen to keep this page one tap away.
Everything you send is encrypted on this phone with your code before it leaves, so the relay service can't read your feed links.
